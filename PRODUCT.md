# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS, deployed to Vercel — user-specified in the rebuild brief. Replaces the previous single-page static HTML site.

## Users

Three distinct visitor audiences, mapped to three service tiers:

1. **Tier 1 — Business Growth Foundations** (`/business-solutions`): non-technical small-business owners/operators looking for lead gen, lead nurture, website design, and CRM automation. Outcome-focused, jargon-averse.
2. **Tier 2 — Systems & Automation** (`/automation`): operations-minded buyers who want workflow automation, integrations, AI agents/chatbots, internal dashboards.
3. **Tier 3 — Data & AI Engineering** (`/engineering`): technical buyers (CTOs, eng leads) evaluating AWS/Lambda, event-driven architecture, Spark/Python data engineering, RAG systems, full-stack platform work. This audience judges credibility by technical depth.

## Product Purpose

Cognicore Solutions LLC is a US-based technology consultancy. The site's job is to route each visitor to the tier that matches their sophistication and convert them into an inquiry (contact form → email).

## Positioning

A genuine team/agency (confirmed: "we" is truthful, multiple people deliver work). Spans the full stack from small-business marketing automation up to senior data/AI engineering — the escalating-tier structure IS the positioning: one firm that can meet you at your level.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Cognicore Solutions LLC. Logo at `assets/logo.png`. Tagline in prior use: "Smart Solutions for a Smarter Business".
- **Pinned visual world (user brief, binding):** terminal/CLI-inspired — monospace headings, dark background, high contrast, sparing scanline/CRT-glow texture; neural-network node-and-edge motifs as abstract background/divider texture. Visual sophistication escalates Tier 1 → Tier 3 (Tier 1 cleanest/most readable, Tier 3 full terminal + network density). Premium and intentional, never hacker-movie parody. Legibility outranks aesthetic.
- Tone: direct, competent, no filler or generic agency-speak.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real outcome claims (user-confirmed genuine): 70% faster application completion on a life-insurance digital onboarding project; 85% order-processing efficiency improvement on an e-commerce automation project.
- Real case studies exist and will be supplied by the user (one per tier minimum). Until supplied, case-study layouts ship with clearly labeled [PLACEHOLDER] content — nothing invented ships as real.
- **Do not fabricate:** client logos, statistics beyond the two confirmed above, testimonials.

## Product Principles

1. Route by sophistication: each tier page speaks its audience's language — Tier 1 outcomes, Tier 3 architecture.
2. Prove, don't claim: real projects and real tooling over adjectives.
3. Truth over polish: placeholders are labeled; nothing fake ships as real.
4. Legibility outranks aesthetic everywhere.
